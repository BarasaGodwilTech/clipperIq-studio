Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

1. Introduction

ClipperIQ Studio ("we", "our", or "the Service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our creator automation platform.

2. Data Processing Approach

ClipperIQ Studio is designed with privacy in mind. Our architecture prioritizes client-side processing:

  • Video Processing: All video processing is performed locally in your browser using FFmpeg.wasm. Your videos are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Storage: Your data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB, including video blobs, clip metadata, and scheduled posts.
  • API Credentials: Your API keys and OAuth tokens are stored locally with XOR encryption and are never transmitted to our servers.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Data Stored Locally (Browser)

  • Uploaded video files and generated clips
  • Clip metadata and analysis scores
  • Scheduled post information and captions
  • API credentials (encrypted) for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube
  • OAuth tokens and refresh tokens (encrypted)
  • User preferences and settings

3.2 Data Transmitted to Third-Party Platforms

When you publish content, the following data is sent directly to the respective platforms:

  • TikTok: Video files, captions, and publishing metadata via TikTok API
  • Instagram: Video files, captions, and publishing metadata via Instagram Graph API
  • YouTube: Video files, captions, and publishing metadata via YouTube Data API

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Process and generate video clips using client-side FFmpeg.wasm
  • Schedule and publish content to connected social media platforms
  • Authenticate with third-party platforms via OAuth
  • Store your preferences and settings locally
  • Provide the core functionality of the Service

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB
  • API credentials and OAuth tokens are encrypted using XOR encryption
  • We do not have access to your locally stored data
  • Clearing your browser data will delete all stored information
  • We recommend regularly backing up important data

6. Third-Party Services

ClipperIQ Studio integrates with third-party platforms and services:

  • TikTok API: Used for authentication and video publishing
  • Instagram Graph API: Used for authentication and video publishing
  • YouTube Data API: Used for authentication and video publishing
  • FFmpeg.wasm (CDN): Loaded from unpkg.com for client-side video processing

Your use of these third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

ClipperIQ Studio uses browser local storage and IndexedDB to store your data and preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services.

8. Data Retention

Your data remains stored locally in your browser until you:

  • Clear your browser data
  • Uninstall the application
  • Manually delete data through the application interface

9. Your Rights

Since your data is stored locally in your browser, you have full control:

  • You can access all your data through the application interface
  • You can delete your data at any time
  • You can export your data by using browser developer tools
  • You can revoke API access through the respective platform settings

10. Children's Privacy

ClipperIQ Studio is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. International Data Transfers

Since data is processed and stored locally in your browser, international data transfers are determined by your location and the third-party platforms you connect to.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Information

For questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the application.
