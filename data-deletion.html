ClipperIQ Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 6, 2026

1. Overview

ClipperIQ Studio is designed with privacy in mind. Your data is primarily stored locally in your browser, giving you full control over your information. This page explains how you can delete your data from ClipperIQ Studio and request deletion from connected third-party platforms.

2. Local Data Deletion

Since ClipperIQ Studio stores your data locally in your browser, you can delete your data at any time using the following methods:

2.1 Through the Application

  • Open ClipperIQ Studio
  • Navigate to Settings or Preferences
  • Select "Clear All Data" or "Delete Local Storage"
  • Confirm the deletion action

2.2 Through Browser Settings

  • Open your browser's settings
  • Navigate to Privacy & Security
  • Find "Site Settings" or "Cookies and Site Data"
  • Locate clipperiqstudio.ezeemoney.site
  • Select "Clear Data" or "Remove"

2.3 Types of Local Data Deleted

When you delete your local data, the following will be removed:

  • Uploaded video files and generated clips
  • Clip metadata and analysis scores
  • Scheduled post information and captions
  • API credentials (encrypted) for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube
  • OAuth tokens and refresh tokens (encrypted)
  • User preferences and settings

3. Third-Party Platform Data Deletion

ClipperIQ Studio does not store your data on our servers. However, when you publish content to social media platforms, your data is stored on those platforms. To delete your data from third-party platforms, you must contact them directly:

3.1 TikTok Data Deletion

  • Go to TikTok Settings > Privacy > Account
  • Select "Download your data" or "Delete account"
  • Or contact TikTok privacy: TikTok Privacy Center

3.2 Instagram/Facebook Data Deletion

  • Go to Facebook Settings > Your Facebook Information
  • Select "Delete Your Account and Information"
  • Or use the Facebook Account Deletion form
  • For Instagram: Settings > Accounts Center > Personal Details > Account Deletion

3.3 YouTube Data Deletion

  • Go to Google Account Settings > Data & Privacy
  • Select "Delete a Google Service" or "Delete your Google Account"
  • Or use Google Account Deletion

4. Revoking App Access

To disconnect ClipperIQ Studio from your social media accounts without deleting your entire account:

4.1 TikTok

  • Go to TikTok Settings > Security > Manage Apps
  • Find ClipperIQ Studio and select "Remove Access"

4.2 Instagram/Facebook

  • Go to Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites
  • Find ClipperIQ Studio and select "Remove"

4.3 YouTube

  • Go to Google Account Settings > Security > Third-party apps
  • Find ClipperIQ Studio and select "Remove Access"

5. Data Deletion Request

If you have deleted your local data and revoked app access but still have concerns about data stored on third-party platforms, you may contact us for assistance:

  • Email: support@clipperiqstudio.ezeemoney.site
  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request"
  • Please include your account information and the specific platforms you want data deleted from

6. Response Time

We will respond to data deletion requests within 30 days of receipt. However, please note that:

  • ClipperIQ Studio does not store your data on our servers
  • We can only guide you through the deletion process for third-party platforms
  • Final deletion of data from social media platforms is controlled by those platforms

7. GDPR Rights

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate personal data
  • Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing of your personal data
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing of your personal data

8. Contact Information

For questions about data deletion or to submit a formal request, please contact us:

  • Email: support@clipperiqstudio.ezeemoney.site
  • We will respond within 30 days
